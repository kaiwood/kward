# Code of conduct

Kward's community should be welcoming, practical, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about impact.
- Accept correction and make room for others to participate.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Protect private information and never publish another person's credentials, identifying data, or private correspondence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected participants.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact email published in `kward.gemspec`. Do not use a public issue when the report contains private or sensitive information. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
